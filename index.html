The opportunity

Customer support is still too manual.

E-commerce businesses receive thousands of messages involving orders, returns, delivery issues and general enquiries. Teams spend valuable time reading, classifying, prioritising and forwarding these requests.

01

The challenge

High email volumes, repetitive requests, inconsistent categorisation and manual routing create operational overhead and slower customer response times.

02

The approach

Apply AI to understand incoming messages, identify customer intent, assign structured categories and route requests into appropriate workflows.

How it works

A modular AI processing pipeline.

The initial product focuses on email ingestion, classification and routing, with room to extend into more advanced customer operations.

STEP 01

Receive incoming messages through email integrations.

STEP 02

Understanding

Extract relevant information and classify customer intent.

STEP 03

STEP 04

Processing

Execute the corresponding workflow with reliable processing.
